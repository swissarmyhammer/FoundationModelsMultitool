## Goal

Add two read verbs that the sah MCP tool does not have (git.md, "Verbs"). `show` is also the blob reader that `tools.git.diff` uses for `path@ref`.

## Work

1. A shared internal reader: `blob(path:ref:) -> content or correction`. The path goes through the `PathGuard` of `GitContext`. A binary blob is a correction ("binary file"), not text.
2. `Show` verb (`name = "show"`). Arguments: `path: String`, `ref: String?` (default `HEAD`). Result: `path`, `ref`, `content: String?`, `correction: String?`. Keep a size cap in the pattern of `tools.files.read`, and report when the cap cut the content.
3. `Log` verb (`name = "log"`). Arguments: `ref: String?` (default `HEAD`), `path: String?` (only commits that change this path), `limit: Int?` (default and maximum to decide; for example 20 and 200). Result: `commits` (each: `sha`, `shortSha`, `author`, `date` in ISO 8601, `subject`), `capped: Bool`, `correction: String?`.
4. Add both verbs to `GitCapability.tools`.

## Tests

Use the temporary-repository helper.
- `show` at `HEAD`, at an older commit, at a branch name; unknown ref; unknown path; path outside the root; binary file; content over the cap.
- `log` with no arguments, with `limit`, with `path`, on a branch; unknown ref.
