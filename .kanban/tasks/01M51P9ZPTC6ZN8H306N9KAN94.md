title: ProcessState deinit in StdioServerProcess still reaps the child with a blocking waitpid
---
Sources/FoundationModelsMultitool/Capabilities/MCP/StdioServerProcess.swift: the deinit of ProcessState kills and reaps the current child with killpg and waitpid(pid, &status, 0). deinit cannot await, thus it cannot use DedicatedThread.run. When the last reference goes away on a cooperative pool thread, that thread waits until the child exits. The child is killed first, thus the wait is short, but it is a synchronous wait on the pool.

The test releasingEveryReferenceReapsTheProcessViaOwnerTeardown expects that the pid is gone right after `stdio = nil`. A change must keep that order or change the test to wait for an event.

Possible fix: give the reap to a dedicated thread (detached Thread) from deinit and let the test await a reap event from the registry.
