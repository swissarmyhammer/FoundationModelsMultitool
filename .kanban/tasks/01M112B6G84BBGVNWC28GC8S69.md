## What
Port the stdio transport that eventplan.md § "Consolidation of the siblings" names (`StdioServerProcess`). Behavioral port; new header comments in the `//` block style of `Capabilities/Shell/ShellCapability.swift`.

- Target: `Sources/FoundationModelsMultitool/Capabilities/MCP/StdioServerProcess.swift`.

This package uses the shared `FoundationModelsExtras.ProcessRegistry` (see the `extrasDependencyName` comment in `Package.swift` and `Capabilities/Shell/ShellRunner.swift`).
