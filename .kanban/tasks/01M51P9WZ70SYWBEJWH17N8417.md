title: StdioServerProcess reaps a child with a blocking waitpid on a cooperative pool thread
---
Sources/FoundationModelsMultitool/Capabilities/MCP/StdioServerProcess.swift: killReapAndDeregister called killpg and then waitpid(pid, &status, 0) with no WNOHANG. The async paths shutdown, respawn, disconnect and dispose called it on a cooperative pool thread.

Fix: ProcessState.killReapAndDeregister is async. It runs killpg and waitpid on a dedicated thread through DedicatedThread.run, then deregisters the pid. recordSpawn, terminateCurrent and terminateIfCurrent are async, and the transport awaits them. StdioServerTransport is now an internal actor with a nonisolated requestDescriptor, thus a test can read the descriptor.

Test: DedicatedThreadTests.theBodyRunsWithNoCurrentTask proves that the body runs with no current task (fails as a check on the old inline shape). The existing StdioServerProcessTests prove that the reap still occurs.

Not fixed here: deinit still reaps synchronously, because deinit cannot await. See the follow-up card.
