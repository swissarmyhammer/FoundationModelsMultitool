## What

Direction of the user on card ^zqz1zan, part 4: the selection prompt holds only the descriptions of the tools, not the full parameter text. The seam exists. `SearchableMetadata.renderSummaryBlock()` (registry) seeds the selection prefix (`SelectionTier.candidateEntry(forID:catalog:)` in the ranker), and `APISurface.Entry` leaves it at the protocol default, the full `block` (`Sources/FoundationModelsMultitool/Surface/APISurface+SearchableMetadata.swift` lines 17-19). The full block carries the JSDoc `@param`, `@returns` and `@example` lines and the `declare function` line.

Measured on 2026-09-09 over the surface of the agent (files writable, shell on; 9 entries):

- Full prefix: 17,263 chars (preamble `SearchToolsTool.selectionPreamble` 356 chars; `SelectionTier.assemblePrefix`).
- Description-only summaries (`## <path>` heading, `// tools.<path>` banner, the doc lines before the first `@` tag): 7,232 chars in total. A description-only prefix is about 7,600 chars: 44% of the prefix today and 24% of the 32,000 budget. Per entry: read 695, write 583, edit 843, patch 1,576, glob 633, grep 843, execute 935, getLines 527, grepHistory 579.

## What to change

- `ToolDescriptor` gets a `description` field. The renderer has the text (`commentLines(for: description)` in `ToolAPIRenderer.swift` line 249), so no parsing of the JSDoc is needed.
- `APISurface.Entry.renderSummaryBlock()` returns the `// tools.<path>` banner and the description only.
- `renderBlock()` stays the full block: `SearchToolsTool` splices the full block for the selected ids, so the main session keeps the signatures.
- `MultitoolCLI/DemoTools.swift` (`summary`) follows the same rule.
- Keep `AgentSurfaceDiscoveryTests` green on `mlx-community/Qwen3-4B-4bit` (ten queries; 4 to 9 must answer). Paste the counts before and after on this card.
